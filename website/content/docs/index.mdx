Xilo is a desktop app for Anker SOLIX power stations, Solarbanks and chargers. It shows the battery and the power on every port, changes every setting, and runs automations, all over Bluetooth. You don't need an Anker account or an internet connection.

## What Xilo does

- **Dashboards** with the battery, temperature, time left and the power on every port. The C300X and C1000 have dashboards like the ones in the Anker app.
- **Every setting** of the Anker app, such as the AC charging power and the timeouts. Xilo checks each value before it sends it.
- **Automations** that switch outputs on a schedule, keep a battery reserve and send alerts.
- **History** of the battery and power, kept on your computer, with CSV export.
- **The menu bar** shows the battery level and has quick switches.
- **A command line tool**, `xilo`, for scripts.

Xilo is not made by or affiliated with Anker.
