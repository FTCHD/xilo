## Dashboard

The header shows the battery level, the temperature and the time until the battery is full or empty. The dashboard below it shows:

- **Total input and output**, in watts.
- **Ports**: every USB-C, USB-A, solar and AC port, with its power and whether it's an input or an output.
- **Outputs**: the AC output and the car output, with a switch, a live chart and a timer.
- **Controls**: the light and the screen.

The C300 and C1000 families have dashboards made for them, like the ones in the Anker app. Other models use the general dashboard, which shows the power in and out, the controls and every value the device reports. See the page of your [device](/docs/devices).

If the device reports an error, the dashboard shows it with the text from the Anker app.

## Settings

The **Settings** tab has every setting of the Anker app for your model, with the same options. For example, on a C300X:

- AC recharging power: 100, 200, 300 or 330 W
- AC output frequency: 50 or 60 Hz
- Device timeout, screen timeout and screen brightness
- Car charger power saving, smart AC output and port memory

Xilo checks every value against the limits of your model before it sends it, because the device itself accepts values it can't handle. Settings that are hard to undo ask first, such as the AC frequency and **Restore Defaults**.

The **General** card shows the device name, the model, the serial number and the firmware version. Xilo shows the firmware version but never changes the firmware.
