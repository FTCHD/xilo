title: History
description: Xilo records the battery and power of your Anker SOLIX devices every 10 seconds, on your computer, with charts, energy per day and CSV export.
---

<Screenshot src="/screenshots/history.webp" alt="Battery and power history in Xilo" />

Xilo records the battery level and the power of each device every 10 seconds while it's connected. The **History** tab shows:

- the battery level over 24 hours, 7 days or 30 days
- the power in and out
- the energy in and out for each day, in watt-hours

Click **Export CSV** to save the history of the chosen period as a spreadsheet file.

The history is stored only on your computer. In **Preferences**, you can turn recording off, and choose to keep it for 30 days, 90 days or a year.
