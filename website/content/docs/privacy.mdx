---
title: Privacy
description: Xilo talks to your Anker SOLIX devices over Bluetooth only. It has no account, sends nothing to the internet, and keeps your settings and history on your computer.
---

Xilo is private by design.

- **No account.** There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to sign in to.
- **Bluetooth only.** Xilo talks to your devices directly. It doesn't use Anker's cloud or any other server.
- **No internet.** Xilo works without an internet connection, for example during a power cut.
- **Your data stays on your computer.** Your settings, the history and the logs are stored only there.

## What Xilo stores

| What | Where on macOS |
| --- | --- |
| Settings and your devices | `~/Library/Application Support/dev.xilo.Xilo/config.toml` |
| History | `~/Library/Application Support/dev.xilo.Xilo/history.sqlite` |
| Logs | `~/Library/Application Support/dev.xilo.Xilo/logs` |

On Windows and Linux, the same files are in the usual folder for app data.

To remove a device and its history, use **Settings → Remove device**.
