title: FAQ
description: Answers about Xilo, the free desktop app for Anker SOLIX power stations on macOS, Windows and Linux.
---

## Is Xilo free?

Yes. Xilo is free to download and use on macOS, Windows and Linux.

## Does Xilo need an Anker account or the internet?

No. Xilo talks to your devices over Bluetooth only. There is no account, and it works without an internet connection.

## Which devices does Xilo support?

38 Anker models: SOLIX power stations such as the C300X, C1000 and F3800, Solarbanks, the Home Power Panel, Prime chargers and EverFrost coolers. See [Supported devices](/docs/devices).

## Can I use Xilo and the Anker app at the same time?

A device accepts one Bluetooth connection at a time. Choose **Release to phone** in Xilo when you want to use the Anker app.

## Does Xilo work with devices on Wi-Fi?

Xilo uses Bluetooth only. A device that you set up on Wi-Fi in the Anker app still works with Xilo, as long as it accepts Bluetooth connections.

## Does Xilo update the firmware?

No. Xilo shows the installed firmware version, but never changes it.

## Is it safe to change settings with Xilo?

Xilo checks every value against the limits of your model before it sends it, and settings that are hard to undo ask first.

## Is Xilo made by Anker?

No. Xilo is an independent app. It is not made by or affiliated with Anker. Anker and SOLIX are trademarks of Anker Innovations.
