Xilo connects to the Anker Prime Charger 160W (A2687) over Bluetooth, with no account, cloud or internet. Xilo shows the Prime Charger 160W on its general dashboard: the power in and out, the controls, and every value the device reports.

## What Xilo shows

<Columns>

- USB-C1
- USB-C1 voltage (V)
- USB-C1 current (A)
- USB-C1 power (W)
- USB-C2
- USB-C2 voltage (V)
- USB-C2 current (A)
- USB-C2 power (W)
- USB-C3
- USB-C3 voltage (V)
- USB-C3 current (A)
- USB-C3 power (W)

</Columns>

## What you can change

### Controls

| | Values |
| --- | --- |
| USB-C1 | On or off |
| USB-C2 | On or off |
| USB-C3 | On or off |

Xilo checks every value against these limits before it sends anything, because the device itself accepts values it can't handle.

## Automations

- **Schedules** can switch USB-C1, USB-C2 and USB-C3 at set times, on the days you choose.
- **Alerts** for connection.

See [Automations](/docs/automations) for how they work.

## Connection

Xilo talks to the Prime Charger 160W with Anker's Prime Bluetooth protocol, encrypted. It finds the device by itself when you scan, and remembers it. The model number is A2687.

[Download Xilo](/docs/install) for macOS, Windows or Linux.
