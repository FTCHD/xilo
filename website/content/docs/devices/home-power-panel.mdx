Xilo connects to the Anker SOLIX Home Power Panel (A17B1) over Bluetooth, with no account, cloud or internet. Xilo shows the Home Power Panel on its general dashboard: the power in and out, the controls, and every value the device reports.

## Automations

This model has no controls that a schedule can switch. Alerts still work: errors, battery levels, AC power and connection.

See [Automations](/docs/automations) for how they work.

## Connection

Xilo talks to the Home Power Panel with Anker's ZX Bluetooth protocol, encrypted. It finds the device by itself when you scan, and remembers it. The model number is A17B1.
