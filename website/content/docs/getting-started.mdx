## Before you start

A device accepts one Bluetooth connection at a time. If the Anker app on your phone is connected to it, close the Anker app or turn off Bluetooth on your phone first.

Keep the device within a few meters of your computer, and make sure it's switched on.

## Add the device

1. Open Xilo and click **Add Device** in the sidebar.
2. Xilo scans for nearby Anker devices. Each one shows its model and its signal strength.
3. Click **Add** next to your device.

<Screenshot src="/screenshots/add-device.webp" alt="The Add Device page in Xilo, with nearby Anker SOLIX devices" />

Xilo connects, detects the model and opens the dashboard. It remembers the device and connects again by itself next time.

If Xilo detects the wrong model, choose the right one in **Settings → Model**.

## Rename the device

Give the device a name you recognize, such as "Desk C300X", in **Settings → Device Name**. The name is only stored in Xilo.

## Use the Anker app again

When you want to use the Anker app on your phone, choose **Release to phone** in the device's settings or in the menu bar. Xilo lets go of the device until you connect again.
