Each device has an **Automations** tab. Automations run while Xilo is open, or in the menu bar or system tray, and the device is in Bluetooth range.

<Screenshot src="/screenshots/automations.webp" alt="The Automations tab of an Anker SOLIX C300X in Xilo" />

## Schedules

A schedule turns the AC output, the car output or the screen on or off, or sets the light, at a set time on the days you choose. Click **Add Schedule**, choose the control, the value, the time and the days, then **Add**.

You can pause a schedule with its switch, without deleting it.

If the device is out of range when a schedule is due, Xilo waits up to 10 minutes. After that, it notes the schedule as missed in the Activity list.

## Battery reserve

When the battery drops to 10, 20, 30 or 50% while it discharges, Xilo turns off the outputs you choose. This keeps some charge for when you need it.

If you turn an output on again, Xilo leaves it on until the battery charges above the reserve. A schedule doesn't turn an output on while the battery is at the reserve.

## USB wake

Some devices, like the C300X, turn their USB ports off after about two hours of low load. A phone that trickle-charges overnight then stops charging.

USB wake switches the car output on briefly before that happens, which keeps the ports on. If the car output was off, Xilo turns it off again 3 seconds later. You can choose when it wakes the ports, and a battery level below which it doesn't.

## Alerts

Xilo shows alerts in the app and as system notifications:

- errors that the device reports
- low battery, at 10, 20 or 30%
- charged, at 80, 90 or 100%
- AC power lost, and AC power back
- a device out of reach for 10 minutes

Choose the alerts in **Preferences**.

## Activity

The bottom of the Automations tab lists every automation and alert from the last 7 days.
