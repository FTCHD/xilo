---
title: The menu bar and system tray
description: Keep an eye on your Anker SOLIX power station from the macOS menu bar or the Windows and Linux system tray, switch outputs quickly, and keep Xilo running in the background.
---

Xilo has an icon in the menu bar on macOS, and in the system tray on Windows and Linux. Its menu shows each device with its battery level and power. From there you can:

- switch the AC and car outputs on or off
- release every device, so the Anker app on your phone can connect
- open the Xilo window

## Run in the background

With **Keep running in the menu bar** (or **in the system tray**) on in **Preferences**, closing the window keeps Xilo connected. Your automations and alerts keep working.

Turn on **Open at login** to start Xilo when you log in to your computer. It works on macOS, Windows and Linux.

On some Linux desktops, such as GNOME, tray icons need an extension, for example AppIndicator support.
