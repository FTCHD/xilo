---
title: The menu bar
description: Keep an eye on your Anker SOLIX power station from the menu bar, switch outputs quickly, and keep Xilo running in the background.
---

Xilo shows each device in the menu bar, with its battery level and power. From there you can:

- switch the AC and car outputs on or off
- release every device, so the Anker app on your phone can connect
- open the Xilo window

## Run in the background

With **Keep running in the menu bar** on in **Preferences**, closing the window keeps Xilo connected. Your automations and alerts keep working.

Turn on **Open at login** to start Xilo with your computer.
